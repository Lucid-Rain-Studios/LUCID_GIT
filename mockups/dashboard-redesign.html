What changed and why

  1. One header, one Fetch. Fetch showed up three times (top bar, greeting, Sync card). Now it lives in the Sync step only; the header carries just status and auto-fetch.
  2. Suggestions are a slim full-width row. Advice sits above the steps it refers to, one line each, and no longer takes a tall card.
  3. Sync + Conflicts + PR merged into one flow. Left to right: where you are, whether it is safe, what to do next. The next step is highlighted, so the primary action is always obvious.
  4. Local changes can commit in place. Select files, type a message, commit. Before, the card was read-only and "View All" was the only way forward.
  5. PRs sit under Local changes. Left column is the "act now" column: your changes, then PRs waiting on review. Short list, never needs scrolling.
  6. Locks sit on the right above the graph. A long, scrollable list pairs better with the tall graph card. It gets search and bulk unlock: 33 own and 105 team locks are unusable without a filter. Unlock all is danger-styled and should ask to confirm.
  7. Two-month graph fills its card. Cells scale to the width. Older months load lazily via ‹ (skeleton shown while loading, cached after). Stats cover the loaded range, so numbers match the grid.
